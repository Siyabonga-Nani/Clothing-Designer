Clothing Designer

Tools Guide

Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y)

Standard shortcuts to reverse or re-apply your last actions.

Draw (D)

Click to add points. They automatically connect into segments.

Move (M) & Select (S)

Drag to adjust. Hold Shift/Ctrl to select and move multiple points at once.

Smooth Bending (M/B)

Moving an anchor point also moves its handles, preserving local curve smoothness.

Grading (E) & Sizes

Define X/Y increments in the Inspector. Toggle XS-XXL in the Topbar to see scaling.

Cut Segment (C)

Split any line into two independent segments.

Curve (B)

Click any segment to add Bezier handles. Drag handles to refine shape.

Mirror (I)

Reflect your pattern across a selected line (e.g., Center Front).

3D View (4)

Left-Drag to Rotate, Right-Drag to Pan. Click an anchor to snap focus.

Measure (R)

Drag between points to see exact production distances.

Fabric (F)

Click 4 points to place a fabric piece. Select it to change type or cut it to the pattern.
